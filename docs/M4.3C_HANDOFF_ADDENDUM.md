# Corporation Helix — M4.3C Handoff Addendum
**Date:** 2026-09-06  
**Milestone:** M4.3C — Iterative Evidence Expansion

## Decision
M4.3B proved the evidence-bearing domain candidate contract. M4.3C adds a provider-agnostic recursive expansion layer.

> **Helix recursively expands accepted knowledge, not guesses.**

Zero/greenfield, customer-supplied, and existing-ASM starts are the same workflow with different Iteration 0 seeds.

## Fact contract
`fact_type`, `value`, `subject`, `identifier`, `source`, `confidence`, `status`,
`pivot_eligible`, `iteration_discovered`, `evidence`, `metadata`.

Only `ACCEPTED + HIGH + pivot_eligible=true` facts may enter the next frontier.
REVIEW/REJECTED facts remain auditable but cannot recursively expand.

## Loop
Iteration 0 seeds → applicable isolated providers → observations/candidates →
existing evidence policy → new trusted facts → next frontier → repeat until no
new trusted pivots. A maximum-iteration guard prevents runaway expansion.
Provider failures are recorded per pivot and do not abort the run.
